BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little more clarity.

What information should I include in my brief?+

A location, approximate area, intended use, preferred timeline, and budget range are useful starting points. Tell us what you know so far; you can discuss drawings and detailed requirements in a follow-up conversation.

Can I enquire before I have drawings?+

Yes. Describe your idea and current stage, whether you have a detailed brief or are still exploring the possibilities.

Are the projects on this website completed company projects?+

The current portfolio contains illustrative concepts and reference imagery. Verified project details and original photography will replace them before the company’s public launch.
