How information is handled on this concept website.

This website currently offers a demonstration enquiry form. The information below explains how your entries are handled.

Contact enquiries

The form asks for your name, email address, service interest, message, and consent. You may also share a phone number, company, budget range, project location, and preferred timeline. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

This is a demonstration form. Pressing Send enquiry validates your entries, displays a sample success message, and resets the form. It does not send, upload, or save your details. The success notice is a demonstration of the intended experience, not confirmation of delivery.

Saving a copy

Save a copy prepares a downloadable text file on your device. This action does not send an enquiry. The website does not store your message in browser local storage. Keep or delete the downloaded file as you prefer.

External resources

Architectural reference images load from Unsplash and fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers receive standard connection information, including an IP address, when your browser requests their files. There is no connected contact-form service.

Analytics and hosting

This website does not include advertising trackers or application cookies. Hosting providers may process access logs according to their policies. If published with GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security.

Before public launch

Company identity, privacy contact details, retention practices, and this policy must be confirmed before online enquiry delivery is enabled. The project portfolio and careers listings currently contain illustrative content.
